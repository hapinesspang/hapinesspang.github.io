Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 10, 2026 · Last updated: July 12, 2026

DailyKit works without an account and keeps local-only content on your device. If you choose account connection, sharing, system calendar access, notifications, or ad-supported use, the information needed for those features is processed as described below.

This Privacy Policy explains how Yoobin Shin (hapinesspang) (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information in connection with the DailyKit app (“App”).

1. Information we process

Content you create

  • Calendar events created in DailyKit.
  • Finance data such as transactions, budgets, accounts, categories, assets, and related settings.
  • App preferences, selected calendar options, and other content you configure.
  • Legacy notes or reminders data retained from an earlier version or legacy synced store.

Feature and account information

  • Calendar and notification permission status.
  • Server user, device, space, membership, invite, and sync identifiers; access tokens; sync cursors; sharing roles; and related connection settings when optional server features are used.
  • Device display names and platform information used for connected-device and sharing management.
  • Legacy iCloud or CloudKit information needed to support or clean up an older sync mode.
  • Push-notification registration and delivery information for shared-content updates.
  • Ad-free promotion codes you submit and the resulting local ad-removal status.

Advertising information

When ads are enabled, Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), and related advertising or measurement partners may process IP address; device and app information; approximate location inferred from network information; ad interactions; advertising, performance, crash, and diagnostic data; and consent or privacy choice status. Device information may include IDFA only when App Tracking Transparency permission is granted.

2. How we use information

  • Provide calendar, finance, settings, backup, restoration, sync, and sharing features.
  • Display selected system calendar events when you enable calendar integration.
  • Deliver local reminders and shared-content update notifications.
  • Manage device linking, access, invite codes, memberships, and sharing permissions.
  • Verify ad-free promotion codes and maintain local ad-removal status.
  • Display contextual or, when permitted, personalized ads, manage advertising privacy choices, and measure ad performance.
  • Meet legal obligations, prevent abuse, and protect the App and its users.

3. Permissions, legal basis, and consent

Where applicable law requires it, we rely on your consent or device permission for system calendar access, notifications, advertising privacy choices managed through Google UMP, and IDFA access through App Tracking Transparency. You can change calendar, notification, or tracking permission in iOS Settings and reopen available ad privacy choices from DailyKit Settings → Ads & Privacy.

4. Storage and retention

  • Local content generally remains until you delete it or remove the App.
  • Server-synced content may remain until you delete it, stop sharing, leave a shared space, revoke a device, disable sync, or delete the optional account, subject to operational backup retention.
  • Legacy iCloud or CloudKit content follows the controls and retention behavior of Apple’s platform services.
  • Sharing metadata remains while the relevant share, invite, membership, or shared space is active.
  • Advertising and consent data is retained according to the relevant provider’s policies and legal duties.
  • Limited records may be retained for security, fraud prevention, debugging, dispute handling, or legal compliance.

5. When information is shared

We do not sell personal information in the ordinary sense of exchanging it for money. Information may be made available:

  • To Apple for App Store distribution, EventKit, legacy iCloud or CloudKit, APNs, and related platform functions.
  • To Supabase and the infrastructure used to operate optional DailyKit server sync and sharing.
  • To people you intentionally share supported calendar or finance spaces with.
  • To Google and related advertising or measurement partners when advertising is enabled.
  • When required by law or needed to protect rights, safety, or App integrity.

6. International processing

Apple, Supabase, Google, and related infrastructure providers may process information in the United States or other countries where they operate. Such processing is subject to the providers’ policies, contracts, and applicable safeguards.

7. Advertising and UMP

DailyKit may use Google AdMob to provide free, ad-supported use. The current release supports a consent-based personalized-ad path while keeping every calendar, finance, saving, sync, sharing, and settings feature available without tracking permission.

  • Google UMP requests, stores, and manages applicable advertising privacy choices before eligible ads are requested.
  • When applicable, UMP presents the AdMob IDFA explainer immediately before Apple’s App Tracking Transparency alert.
  • Only when the applicable UMP choice permits it and ATT is authorized may IDFA be used for personalized ads and ad measurement.
  • If ATT is denied, restricted, unavailable, or not yet determined, DailyKit forces non-personalized ad treatment and Google Mobile Ads does not receive IDFA from the App. Ads may instead be limited or unavailable.
  • Calendar events, finance records, account content, and shared-space content are never used to personalize advertising.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete information, withdraw consent, or object to certain processing. In DailyKit you can delete individual items, stop sharing, leave spaces, revoke devices, disable sync, change device permissions, and change available advertising privacy choices.

You can permanently delete an optional DailyKit server account from Settings → Account & Sync → Delete Account while connected.

9. Deletion and destruction

DailyKit does not require registration for local-only use. When you delete the optional server account, the account, connected devices, personal server records, owned shared spaces, and memberships in joined spaces are deleted or made inaccessible through server controls. Content stored only on your device remains until you delete it or remove the App.

Electronic records are deleted using commercially reasonable measures. Deletion from backups or third-party platform infrastructure may not be immediate because it follows provider backup schedules and operational processes. If physical records were ever created, they would be destroyed using an appropriate method such as shredding.

10. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children where prohibited by applicable law, and we do not knowingly collect children’s personal information in violation of applicable law. Contact us if you believe information was provided unlawfully.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information processed through DailyKit. No method of transmission, storage, or processing can be guaranteed completely secure.
