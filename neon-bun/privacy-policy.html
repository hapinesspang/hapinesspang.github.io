Neon Bun Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-12

Neon Bun stores game progress and settings on the device. The app has no operator account or developer server and uses Google AdMob advertising plus Firebase Crashlytics. The Firebase Analytics SDK is linked but collection is disabled in version 1.0. When the device is signed in to Game Center, eligible Closing Rush scores are submitted automatically to Apple Game Center.

1. Controller, privacy officer, and purposes

  • Operator and controller: Yoobin Shin (hapinesspang)
  • Privacy officer (CPO): Yoobin Shin (hapinesspang)
  • Privacy requests: hapinesspang@gmail.com

The app processes the minimum information needed to provide its core features, retain user settings and records on the device, deliver ads, understand app usage, diagnose errors, and improve stability. The app does not provide account creation or login, so there is no developer account data to delete.

The app uses Google AdMob ads to remain free and does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions.

2. Information processed on the device

  • Game progress, high scores, tip balance, Daily Shift completion history, one-per-day rewarded 100-tip claim history, and user settings are processed and stored on the device.
  • Completed-run identifiers and counts, eligibility-window time, launch count, background-duration decision state, and recent fullscreen presentation times used to limit result interstitial and App Open frequency are processed on the device, with required values stored in UserDefaults.
