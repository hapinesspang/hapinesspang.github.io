Link Cleaner Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 17, 2026

Information We Do Not Collect

Link Cleaner does not send the original links you check, your warning list, your allow list, or reward status to a developer server. The app does not use a developer account system or a developer-operated backend.

On-Device Processing

Link analysis, tracking-parameter removal, and user rule storage are processed on your device. The Safari extension checks links on webpages, but it does not show ads inside Safari.

Ads

The app may show Google AdMob ads to keep the app free. The app may use Google AdMob, the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and the Google User Messaging Platform SDK for ad delivery, ad measurement, fraud prevention, ad quality, diagnostics, and performance.

Examples of information that may be processed for advertising include approximate location inferred from IP address, device identifiers, the device advertising identifier (IDFA) if allowed by the user, ad impressions, ad interaction information, device and app information, diagnostics, and performance information.

The app may request Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission for personalized ads and ad performance measurement. If you do not allow tracking, you can still use the core link-checking and link-cleaning features. Ads may be non-personalized or limited.

Analytics and Crash Diagnostics

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand basic app usage and diagnose crashes. These services may process app interaction events, device and app information, installation or session identifiers, performance data, crash logs, and related diagnostics. Link Cleaner does not intentionally send the original links you check, cleaned links, URL hosts, tracking parameters, warning-list domains, or allow-list domains to Firebase or to a developer server.

Third-Party Services and International Processing

The app may use the following third-party services, which may be provided by Google LLC or its affiliates:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK
  • Google User Messaging Platform SDK
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics

The app uses these services for ad delivery, personalized ads where allowed, ad performance measurement, app usage analytics, error diagnosis, and stability improvement.

During this processing, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, ad impressions and ad interaction information, product interaction information such as app launches and screen navigation, approximate location inferred from IP address, crash logs, performance data, and other diagnostic information may be processed or stored outside the user's country or region through Google LLC and its subprocessors.

  • Recipient: Google LLC
  • Countries or regions: United States and other countries or regions where Google services are provided
  • Timing and method: transmitted over the network during app use, including ad requests, consent status updates, analytics event transmission, and error or crash reporting
  • Purposes: ad delivery, personalized ads and ad performance measurement, app usage analytics, error diagnosis, and service stability improvement
  • Retention period: for the period determined by Google policies and service settings, or until the processing purpose is fulfilled
  • How to limit processing: users can limit personalized ads and tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT), the ad consent screen where available, and device advertising settings. Some advertising, analytics, and error diagnosis processing may continue in a limited form.

Link Cleaner does not intentionally include original links, cleaned links, URL hosts, tracking parameters, warning-list domains, allow-list domains, or user-entered rule domains in Firebase Analytics events or developer-operated server transmissions.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, see the Google Privacy Policy, Google AdMob, and Firebase.
