CopyBox Support

CopyBox is an iOS app for saving copied text, reusable replies, snippets, and queues, then pasting them from the CopyBox keyboard or Shortcuts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does CopyBox automatically save everything I copy?
No. CopyBox saves text only when you explicitly use a save action in the app, keyboard, Share Sheet, or Shortcuts.

What Shortcuts actions are available?
CopyBox can save the current clipboard, add provided text to the active queue, copy the next queue item, reset queue progress, or open the app.

Why does the keyboard ask for Full Access?
Full Access allows the keyboard to read saved queues and snippets from the shared App Group container and update used-state after insertion. The keyboard does not collect everything you type or send typed content to a developer server.

Are saved snippets uploaded to a server?
No. The current version stores saved snippets on the device and does not use a developer-operated account system or backend.
