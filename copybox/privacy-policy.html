CopyBox Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 9, 2026

Information CopyBox Does Not Collect

CopyBox does not send saved clip text, snippets, queue names, or keyboard input to a developer-operated server. CopyBox does not use its own account system or developer backend.

On-Device Storage

Saved clips and snippets are stored on the device in an iOS App Group container so the main app and the keyboard extension can access the same library. CopyBox may read and save text only when you explicitly use a save action in the app, keyboard, Share Sheet, or Shortcuts.

Keyboard Extension

The CopyBox keyboard lets you insert saved queues and snippets into text fields. Full Access is requested so the keyboard can read saved queues and snippets from the shared App Group container and update used-state after insertion. The keyboard does not collect everything you type or send typed content to a developer server.

Ads

CopyBox may show Google AdMob ads in the main app. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process device identifiers, ad data, usage data, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and ad quality. CopyBox does not provide saved clip text to Google or to a developer server for ad targeting.

Analytics and Crash Reports

CopyBox uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics in the main app to understand aggregate feature usage and diagnose crashes. Analytics events and crash reports do not include saved clip text, snippets, queue names, keyboard input, URLs, email addresses, or manually entered content. Firebase may process app identifiers, device and OS information, product interaction events, crash data, diagnostics, and performance information for analytics and app quality purposes.

Third-Party Services and International Processing

CopyBox may use the following third-party services, which may be provided by Google LLC or its affiliates:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK
  • Google Firebase
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics

CopyBox uses Google LLC services, including Google AdMob, Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google User Messaging Platform SDK, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics, for ad delivery, ad performance measurement, app usage analytics, error diagnosis, and stability improvement.

During this processing, device identifiers, advertising identifiers if allowed by the user, ad impressions and ad interaction information, product interaction information such as app launches and screen navigation, approximate location inferred from IP address, crash logs, performance data, and other diagnostic information may be processed or stored outside the user's country or region through Google LLC and its subprocessors.

  • Recipient: Google LLC
  • Countries or regions: United States and other countries or regions where Google services are provided
  • Timing and method: transmitted over the network during app use, including ad requests, analytics event transmission, and error or crash reporting
  • Purposes: ad delivery, personalized ads and ad performance measurement where allowed by user consent and device settings, app usage analytics, error diagnosis, and service stability improvement
  • Retention period: for the period determined by Google policies and service settings, or until the processing purpose is fulfilled
  • How to limit processing: users can limit personalized ads and tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT), the ad consent screen where available, and device advertising settings. Some advertising, analytics, and error diagnosis processing may continue in a limited form.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, see the Google Privacy Policy, Google AdMob, and Firebase.

Clipboard Access

CopyBox does not continuously monitor or collect the clipboard in the background. The app reads clipboard text when you choose a save action or run a Shortcuts action that saves the clipboard. Shortcuts may also add text you provide, copy the next saved queue item, reset queue progress, or open the app.
