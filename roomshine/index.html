A little brighter after every clear.

Dust, boxes, cargo, shaped boards, and special combinations build a deliberate learning curve. The first 50 levels introduce each idea; the full 300-level catalog is ready from day one.

Thoughtful boards

Plan the clean-up

Swap sculpted, shape-coded clay pieces and watch each match pop, cascade, and settle. Every completed puzzle remains open for replay, with stars, score, and remaining-move bests saved locally.

Fair by design

No lives. Ten Room Rush finales.

Most levels have no clock. Each room ends with one generous Room Rush, and an optional rewarded video can add five moves or 30 seconds after the matching failure. Completion ads stay at natural transitions, while Retry and Exit remain available.
